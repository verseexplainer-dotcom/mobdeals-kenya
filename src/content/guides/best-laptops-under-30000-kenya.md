Under Ksh 30,000 you can realistically get:

## What Specs Actually Matter at This Budget?

### RAM: Go for 8GB minimum
4GB RAM was fine in 2016, but modern browsers, Microsoft Office and video calls will slow it down quickly. All the picks above come with 8GB — do not settle for less.

### Storage: SSD is non-negotiable
A laptop with an SSD (Solid State Drive) will feel dramatically faster than one with an HDD (Hard Disk Drive) of the same spec. All our recommended picks above come with SSDs.

### Processor: i5 is the sweet spot
An Intel Core i5 from the 6th or 7th generation (released 2015–2016) is still a capable processor for everyday tasks in 2026. Avoid Core i3 machines if you can — the difference in speed is noticeable.

### Battery: Check it before you commit
On ex-UK laptops, battery health varies. A good unit will give you 4–6 hours. At MobDeals we advise on battery condition before purchase — feel free to ask on WhatsApp.
