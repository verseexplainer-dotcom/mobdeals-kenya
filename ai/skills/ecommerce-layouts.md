# Ecommerce Layouts

- Design product grids mobile-first: 2 columns on small screens where practical, wider grids on desktop.
- Product cards must show image, category, name, price, stock state, and clear action.
- Keep comparison, specs, and trust content easy to scan.
- Prioritize laptops, smartphones, printers, and desktops.
- Keep checkout/contact flows clear and low-friction.
