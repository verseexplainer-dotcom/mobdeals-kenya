title: Refurbished Phone Checks Before Buying
description: Key checks for refurbished iPhones and Android phones, including battery health, storage, cosmetic condition, accessories, and warranty.
publishedAt: 2026-06-08
draft: false
---

For refurbished phones, inspect the exact unit instead of relying only on the model name. Battery health, storage capacity, screen condition, camera performance, Face ID or fingerprint status, and charging behavior should be confirmed.

Storage is important because many phones cannot be upgraded later. A lower price can become frustrating if the device runs out of space quickly.

Ask what accessories are included, what warranty applies, and whether the phone is locked to any network or account before completing the purchase.
