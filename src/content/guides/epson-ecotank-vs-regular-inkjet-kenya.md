## Introduction

When shopping for a printer in Kenya, you will quickly notice two types of inkjet printers: standard cartridge printers (which are cheaper to buy upfront) and Epson EcoTank printers (which cost more upfront but use refillable ink tanks instead of cartridges).

The question most buyers ask is: which one will cost me less over time?

The answer depends on how much you print — and this guide will help you figure out which one is right for your situation.

---

## How Standard Inkjet Cartridges Work

A standard inkjet printer (like the classic HP DeskJet or older Epson models) uses replaceable ink cartridges. Each cartridge holds a small amount of ink — typically enough for 150–300 pages — and needs replacing when it runs out.

The cartridges themselves are where the cost adds up. In Kenya, a set of colour and black cartridges for a mid-range inkjet can cost Ksh 2,000–4,000 and may only last a month or two for a household or small office that prints regularly.

**Annual running cost estimate (moderate use — ~200 pages/month):**
- Cartridge replacements: approximately Ksh 24,000–40,000 per year

That is the hidden cost most buyers miss when they focus only on the sticker price of the printer.

---

## How Epson EcoTank Printers Work

Epson EcoTank printers (like the popular L3250 and L3151) use large refillable ink tanks rather than cartridges. You pour ink into the tank directly from a bottle. Each bottle of Epson ink costs roughly Ksh 500–900 and can print thousands of pages.

The L3250, for example, is rated for approximately 4,500 pages of black and 7,500 pages of colour from a single set of starter ink bottles. That is a significantly lower cost per page than a cartridge printer.

**Annual running cost estimate (moderate use — ~200 pages/month):**
- Ink bottle refills: approximately Ksh 3,000–6,000 per year

---

## The Real Comparison

| | Standard Inkjet | Epson EcoTank (e.g. L3250) |
|---|---|---|
| Upfront price | Lower — from ~Ksh 8,000 | Higher — from ~Ksh 27,000 |
| Ink cost per page | High — ~Ksh 5–10/page | Low — ~Ksh 0.5–2/page |
| Annual ink cost (200 pages/month) | ~Ksh 24,000–40,000 | ~Ksh 3,000–6,000 |
| Break-even point | — | Approximately 6–12 months |
| Best for | Very low print volume | Regular home or office printing |

The EcoTank costs more to buy, but the savings on ink mean it typically pays for itself within 6 to 12 months for anyone printing 100+ pages per month. After that, the savings are ongoing.

---

## Who Should Buy an EcoTank?

The Epson EcoTank makes strong sense for:

**Small businesses and home offices** that print invoices, reports, letters or marketing materials regularly. The economics work in your favour quickly.

**Schools and tutors** who print reading materials, worksheets or lesson notes. Volume printers will recover the cost in weeks.

**Cyber cafés** where printing is a service you offer customers. The low cost per page protects your margin.

**Households** where multiple family members use the printer for school assignments, documents or photos.

---

## Who Is Better Off with a Standard Inkjet?

A standard cartridge inkjet is a reasonable choice if:

- You print very infrequently — a few pages per month. The low upfront cost makes more sense if the printer mostly sits idle.
- You need a small, portable printer for occasional use.
- Your budget does not stretch to EcoTank prices and you cannot wait for the break-even.

That said — if you find yourself replacing cartridges more than twice a year, the EcoTank will save you money.
